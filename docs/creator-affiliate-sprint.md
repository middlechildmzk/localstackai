## Editorial rules

- Do not quote commission rates until verified on the official partner page.
- Do not rank tools by commission.
- Add disclosure near the first monetized CTA.
- Label sponsored placements clearly.
- Use “researched,” “mapped,” or “editorial pick” unless hands-on testing happened.
- Never promise income or views.
- Add last-verified dates before publishing detailed pricing/commission claims.
